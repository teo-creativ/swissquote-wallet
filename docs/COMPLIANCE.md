## Gaps, ordered by importance

1. **Consents are not recorded with a date (point 6).** The legal texts say the accepted version and date are recorded. Today only the mock runs and it stores no date. Needed: the live `POST /account/register` writing `consents.accepted_at`, one version constant shared by the screen and the legal files, and the lawyer's consent stored at account creation.
2. **Swissquote logo and unknown image sources (point 19).** The logo is a third-party trademark used without a recorded permission. The legal notice now says it is shown for demonstration only. Decide before submission whether to keep it, and record the source of the hand photo and coin art or replace them.
3. **NOTICE and README are unfinished (points 8, 19).** `NOTICE` needs the copyright line and licence reference of the SLL packages; the README needs the third-party table (or a link to `docs/THIRD_PARTIES.md`) and the licence paragraph. Both are submission deliverables (CDC 13.1).
4. **Countries of processing are unknown (point 8).** Fill the Vercel and Supabase regions of the three projects in `docs/THIRD_PARTIES.md`; the privacy policy depends on it.
5. **Lawyer portal still labels its legal pages "Draft, placeholder" (point 1)** and has no legal notice or cookies page (points 4, 16).
6. **Swissquote console (points 5, 16).** It sets a cookie but shows no cookie line, no link to the legal pages, and a shorter banner without "Not an official Swissquote product".
7. **No deletion request path (point 20).** At least a visible "Delete my data" entry in Security that explains the limits and how to contact the team.
8. **Accessibility is not verified (points 13, 14, 15).** Run a keyboard pass and a contrast check on the main flows; decide whether the welcome illustration is decorative; note the white-on-orange contrast of 3.2:1 as a known limit.
9. **No guard test for the client legal texts (point 12).** The lawyer texts have one; `content/legal/*.md` do not.
