# Terms of use

> SQ Keys is a student prototype built for the Swissquote Wallet Challenge (BSA x EPFL hackathon, October 2026). It is not an official Swissquote product, runs on a test network only and holds no real funds. These terms have not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

## 1. Who we are and what this is

SQ Keys is experimental software that lets a person hold their own keys for a crypto wallet on a test blockchain (Base Sepolia). It is published by the student team behind the project. Swissquote has no role in these terms. Where the interface shows Swissquote services, they are simulated and marked "Simulated".

## 2. Self-custody software, not a bank service

SQ Keys is software. The key that controls your wallet is split into three shares, and two of them are needed to sign. You hold one share on your device. An independent lawyer holds a second one. A simulated Swissquote service holds the third, for recovery only. No single party can move your assets alone.

We do not hold, manage or invest your assets. We do not provide custody, banking, investment, legal, tax or any other advice.

## 3. Eligibility

The prototype is meant for adult clients whose identity has already been verified by Swissquote. You must be at least 18 years old and have legal capacity.

## 4. Your responsibilities

- You use the wallet at your own risk and under your own responsibility.
- You keep your device, its biometrics and your exit kit secure, and you keep your exit kit passphrase separate from them.
- You check every recipient and amount before you confirm. Transactions are final and cannot be reversed.
- You decide alone whether to use third-party applications. Choosing an application, including one on a list of known applications, is your own decision and not a recommendation.
- You do not use SQ Keys for anything unlawful, including evading sanctions.

## 5. Transfers, limits and checks

Your lawyer may set limits for your account, such as an amount per transfer and a number of transfers per day. A transfer above a limit, or to a recipient not yet approved, needs your lawyer's approval. A transfer that is not approved within 48 hours is cancelled and nothing is written to the blockchain. Transfers to addresses on a sanctions list are refused. These checks reduce risk. They do not guarantee that a transfer is safe or lawful.

## 6. Network fees

Fees on the test network are paid by a simulated Swissquote service. The fee is shown before you sign. Nothing is charged to you in this prototype.

## 7. No purchases and no refunds

There is no purchase in this prototype. Blockchain transactions are irreversible, so there is no refund mechanism.
