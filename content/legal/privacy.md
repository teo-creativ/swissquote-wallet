# Privacy policy

> SQ Keys is a student prototype. This policy explains the data it uses. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

## 1. Who is responsible

The student team that publishes SQ Keys is responsible for the data described here. Contact: through the repository page of the project.

## 2. Principle: as little data as possible

- Your Swissquote client number and your identity documents never reach the wallet application or the lawyer portal. They stay with the simulated Swissquote service. The wallet uses an anonymous reference instead.
- Your fingerprint or face data never leaves your device. Your device only tells the application that you were recognised.
- The application does not use email for security messages.

## 3. What we process

- **Account data:** the anonymous reference, a display name you choose, the status of your account, the date and version of the documents you accepted.
- **Wallet data:** your public wallet address, the public key, and your transactions. Public blockchain data is visible to everyone.
- **Technical data:** device name, the public part of your passkey, and session information needed to keep you signed in.
- **Process data:** transfer requests, decisions, limits, recovery steps with dates, and alerts.
- **Lawyer data:** your display name, wallet address and history of your requests are shown to the lawyer you invite, so they can do their role. The lawyer's name, firm and passkey are kept by the lawyer portal. The lawyer processes this data under their own professional duties and is responsible for that processing.
- **Key shares:** each party stores only its own encrypted share. A share alone does not reveal your key.

## 4. Why we process it

To create and operate your wallet, to check limits and sanctions, to run recovery, to keep a record of what happened, and to protect the service from abuse. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## 5. Records

A record of key actions is kept so that tampering can be detected. It is not edited after the fact.

## 6. Cookies

We use only cookies that are strictly necessary for the application to work, such as the session cookie. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

## 7. Service providers and locations

The prototype uses third-party infrastructure: a web hosting provider, a database provider and a public blockchain node provider. Some providers may process data outside Switzerland; the list is in the project's README. Where data leaves Switzerland, we rely on an adequate level of protection or contractual safeguards, as provided by article 16 of the Federal Act on Data Protection.

## 8. Storage

Prototype data is kept only as long as the project needs it for the competition and for testing, and is then deleted. Data written to a public blockchain cannot be erased.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, or a copy in a usable format, and you can withdraw consents. Because blockchain data cannot be erased, deletion applies to everything else. Some data may have to be kept where a law requires it. To exercise a right, contact the team. You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

## 10. Security

We use encrypted connections, signed requests between services, separate storage for each key share and access rules on databases. No system is perfectly secure.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the current version appears in the application.
