# Risk warning

> SQ Keys is a student prototype on a test network. Test assets have no value. Read this page as if the wallet held real money, because that is the situation a real product would face.

## Read this before you use the wallet

Holding your own keys gives you control, and it also gives you responsibility. There is no bank behind a transfer you send by mistake.

## Market and value risk

- Crypto-assets can lose most or all of their value, quickly and without warning.
- Stablecoins such as USDC are meant to hold a stable price, but they can lose their peg or become unavailable.

## Irreversibility

- A transaction on a public blockchain is final. Nobody can cancel it, not you, your lawyer, Swissquote or us.
- If you send assets to a wrong address or on a wrong network, they are probably lost for good.
- Everything you do on the blockchain is public. Your address and your transactions can be seen by anyone.

## Loss and theft of your phone

- If your phone is lost or stolen, a thief cannot sign alone, because a second share is needed. This protection is real but not absolute.
- A thief who also controls your email and tries to start a recovery faces an identity check, a waiting period and a lawyer's approval. Those checks can fail or be bypassed in rare cases.
- Limits on amounts and on the number of transfers per day reduce what a thief could send, but they do not remove the risk.

## Risks linked to the lawyer and to Swissquote

- Your wallet needs two of three shares. Your lawyer and Swissquote together hold two shares. If both acted together against your interest, they could move your assets. This is why recovery has an identity check, a delay you can cancel and a record of every step. These safeguards reduce the risk and do not remove it. If Swissquote and your lawyer both act against you, or are both compromised, a thief does not need to go through recovery: the two shares they hold are enough to sign.
- If your lawyer becomes unavailable, daily signing and recovery may stop until another way is available.
- If Swissquote's services stop permanently, you can still recover your assets with your exit kit and your lawyer, as long as you have created the kit and keep it with its passphrase.
- If two of the three parties fail at once, for example you lose your phone and your lawyer disappears, your assets may be lost permanently.

## Technology risk

- The cryptographic library used for signing has been audited for key generation and signing. Other functions have not been audited, and software always contains bugs.
- The prototype depends on third-party services such as hosting, a database and a blockchain node. An outage can block you temporarily.
- Test networks can be reset or discontinued without notice.

## Legal and regulatory risk

- Rules on crypto-assets, on the role of lawyers and on transfers to self-hosted wallets can change. Some features could be restricted in the future.
- Authorities may classify one of the parties as a custodian of your assets. This could lead to the service being changed, restricted or stopped.
- Transfers may be refused or delayed for compliance reasons, for example a sanctions match.

## Third-party applications

- Allowing an application to spend your assets is a decision with risk. An application can be malicious or contain errors. An unlimited allowance lets the application spend any amount until you cancel it.

## Not advice

Nothing in this prototype is financial, legal or tax advice. Talk to a qualified professional before you take decisions with real assets.
