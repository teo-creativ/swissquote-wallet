# Cookies

> SQ Keys only uses cookies that are strictly necessary for the application to work. This is why you do not see a cookie banner.

## What we use

- **Session cookie:** keeps you signed in after you log in. It cannot be read by scripts in the page and is only sent over an encrypted connection. It expires after a few hours or when you sign out.
- **Browser storage:** the application keeps a few items in your browser's own storage, such as the encrypted part of your key and the progress of a screen you are filling in. This data stays on your device.

## What we do not use

- No advertising cookies.
- No analytics or audience measurement cookies.
- No cookies set by third parties to follow you across websites.

## Legal basis

Under article 45c of the Swiss Telecommunications Act, storing data on a user's device is allowed when the user is informed of the processing, of its purpose and of the way to refuse it. This page gives you that information. Cookies that are strictly necessary to provide a service you asked for do not need a consent banner.

## How to refuse

You can block or delete cookies and browser storage in the settings of your browser. If you do, you cannot stay signed in, and the part of your key stored on this device is deleted. Create your exit kit before you clear the storage of your browser. See the Recovery terms.

## Changes

If the application ever uses a cookie that is not strictly necessary, this page will be updated first and you will be asked for your consent.
